# Systems Thinking

The ability to hold a whole product in your head and reason about how pieces interact. Includes identifying where systems break under load, where security models have gaps, where UX falls apart at edge cases, and where business logic encodes assumptions about to become wrong. The skill that separates great engineers from adequate ones -- and adequate is no longer viable.

synonyms:: systems design understanding, holistic product reasoning, architectural judgment, whole-system reasoning, cross-cutting concern analysis
