# Self-Referential Loop

AI systems being used to build and improve themselves. Codex 5.3 was instrumental in its own creation (identifying inefficiencies, fixing training scripts). Claude Code's codebase is 90%+ written by Claude Code itself. The feedback loop on AI has closed -- AI builds AI, each generation faster than the last.

synonyms:: ai self-improvement loop, recursive improvement, ai bootstrapping, self-building ai, recursive ai development, ai recursive loop, model self-improvement
