# AI-Native Economics

The revenue-per-employee pattern of AI-native startups averaging $3M+ per employee (5-6x SaaS average). Examples: Cursor ($500M ARR, few dozen employees), Midjourney (~$500M, ~100 people). Template: 15 people generating $100M/year. Org charts flatten radically -- small groups exceptional at understanding users, translating to specs, directing AI.

synonyms:: ai-native org model, hyper-leverage economics, ai-era unit economics, revenue-per-employee scaling, ai startup economics, lean ai org model
