# Generalist over Specialist

The industry hiring shift toward people who can think across domains rather than experts in one narrow tech stack. When AI handles implementation, human value lies in understanding the problem space broadly enough to direct implementation correctly. A Kubernetes expert who cannot reason about product implications is less valuable than a generalist who understands systems, users, and business constraints.

synonyms:: cross-domain thinking, t-shaped to wide-shaped shift, generalist hiring preference, domain-spanning engineers, product-minded developers
