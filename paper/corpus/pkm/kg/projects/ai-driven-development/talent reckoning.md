# Talent Reckoning

The collapse of the junior developer pipeline (US: -67% job postings, UK: -46% grad roles) and hollowing of the career ladder. AI automates entry-level work that juniors learned from, breaking the apprenticeship model. The industry needs better engineers not fewer, but 'better' now means generalists with systems thinking, customer intuition, and spec-writing ability rather than narrow tech-stack specialists.

synonyms:: talent pipeline collapse, junior developer crisis, career ladder hollowing, apprenticeship breakdown, developer hiring crisis, entry-level automation impact
