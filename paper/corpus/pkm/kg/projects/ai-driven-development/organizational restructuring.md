# Organizational Restructuring

The elimination or transformation of coordination structures (standups, sprints, code review, QA teams, Jira boards) that were designed for humans building software. When AI implements, these structures become friction rather than support. The entire coordination layer that constitutes the OS of a modern software org gets deleted or radically transformed.

synonyms:: org restructuring, structural shift, org chart flattening, coordination layer elimination, post-ai org design, agile ceremony removal
