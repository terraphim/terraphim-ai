# Deliberate Naivete

StrongDM's principle of intentionally removing inherited Software 1.0 habits and constraints when building for the agentic era. Creating high-fidelity SaaS clones or full dark factories was previously dismissed as economically unfeasible -- engineers self-censored such proposals. Deliberate naivete means asking 'Why am I doing this? (the model should be doing this instead)' and refusing to carry forward assumptions from the pre-agent era. What was unthinkable six months ago becomes routine.

synonyms:: deliberate naivety, fresh-eyes principle, zero-assumption thinking, factory mindset, pre-agent assumption removal
