# Human Capabilities

The set of skills that matter MORE in the dark factory era, not less: domain expertise, systems thinking, customer intuition, specification quality, and the judgment to know what should be built for whom and why. The dark factory does not replace these -- it amplifies them. The constraint moves from 'can we build it' to 'should we build it'.

synonyms:: irreducible human skills, judgment capabilities, ai-era engineering skills, dark factory human layer, non-automatable engineering
