# Orchestrator

Multi-agent orchestration with scheduling, budgeting, and compound review for the dark factory pattern. The AgentOrchestrator manages agent lifecycle: spawn, monitor, capture output, record learnings. Uses reconciliation loop (tick-based), cost tracking, nightwatch drift detection, and PageRank-based issue prioritisation.

synonyms:: agent orchestrator, dark factory, ADF, AI Dark Factory, orchestration, fleet management, terraphim_orchestrator
