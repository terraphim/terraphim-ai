# Quantum-like modelling

Application of the mathematical apparatus of quantum probability and instruments to macroscopic informational, cognitive, organisational, and sociotechnical systems. Not to be confused with physical quantum mechanics — quantum-like modelling concerns structural similarity of behaviour, not microphysical quantum effects. Used when classical methods fail due to the "innocent measurement" assumption breaking down: order effects, incompatibility of measurements, contextual evidence, and nonseparability.

synonyms:: quantum-likeness, QL, quantum-like cognition, quantum probability, quantum modelling
