# Innocent measurement assumption

The classical modelling assumption that observation, measurement, or querying merely "shows" the state of a system without changing or distorting it. Quantum-like modelling begins where this assumption breaks down: when order of questions changes state, different measurements are incompatible, evidence transfer between contexts is non-neutral, or simplifying (coarsening) a state representation loses predictive power. Common failure mode in engineering management: "we simply measured, we just have an indicator" — yet the act of measuring changes what is being measured.

synonyms:: passive observation, harmless read, read-only access myth, measurement neutrality
