# Enacted distributed state evidence

An FPF pattern (C.26.2) addressing how evidence about system state is created through distributed enactment rather than collected from a pre-existing objective state. The evidence emerges from interactions, roles, and processes across boundaries. Direct copying or transferring measurement results between contexts is not neutral — it involves transformations with conditions of possibility and potential losses, in the spirit of constructor-theoretic possible/impossible tasks.

synonyms:: enacted evidence, distributed state, emergent evidence
