# TOGAF (The Open Group Architecture Framework)

An enterprise architecture framework providing a detailed method (ADM) and supporting tools for developing architecture. Maintained by The Open Group Architecture Forum since 1995, based on the US DoD TAFIM.

TOGAF 9.1 structure:
- Part I: Introduction
- Part II: Architecture Development Method (ADM) -- the core cyclic process
- Part III: ADM Guidelines and Techniques
- Part IV: Architecture Content Framework (metamodel, artifacts, deliverables, building blocks)
- Part V: Enterprise Continuum and Tools
- Part VI: Reference Models (TRM, III-RM)
- Part VII: Architecture Capability Framework

ADM phases: Preliminary, A (Vision), B (Business), C (Information Systems), D (Technology), E (Opportunities), F (Migration Planning), G (Implementation Governance), H (Change Management), Requirements Management.

Related: enterprise architecture, ADM, architecture metamodel, ArchiMate
