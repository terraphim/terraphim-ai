# agentic engineering

The discipline of building software by delegating execution to AI agents while the engineer applies judgment at the right moments across multiple concurrent threads. Distinct from "just coding with AI" because the critical last 20% (architectural evaluation, drift detection, merge decisions) is treated as the engineering part of software engineering, not a handoff. Core practices: team lead model, plan-first execution, parallelism with isolated workspaces, and context engineering.

synonyms:: agent-native software development, agentic software engineering, AI agent engineering, agent-driven development
