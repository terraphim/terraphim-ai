# Preference Centre

A centralised user interface through which customers manage their communication preferences across an enterprise's products and services. Customers select delivery mechanisms (SMS, Email, Push Notification) for each type of alert or communication.

Key characteristics:
- Channel-agnostic: supports multiple delivery mechanisms
- Enterprise-wide: aggregates preferences across all business lines
- Customer-owned: preferences belong to the customer record (SCV)
- Browser-based with native app handoff for mobile integration

Related: push notification architecture, Single Customer View, digital outbound management
