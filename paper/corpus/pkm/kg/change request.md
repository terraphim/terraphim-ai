# Change Request (CR)

A formal process for proposing, assessing, and approving changes to an enterprise system or programme. The CR lifecycle typically includes:

1. Outline of Change (Part A): scope, objectives, requirements, originator, sponsor
2. Impact Assessment (Part B): technical, business, cost, timeline, risk assessment across all affected areas
3. Governance Decisions (Part C): Triage CAB, Design Authority, Programme Board approvals

Key roles: Change Owner, Request Originator, Change Sponsor, Solution Architect, Programme Manager.

In regulated environments (e.g., financial services), CRs include mandatory Conduct Risk assessment to ensure fair customer outcomes.

Related: enterprise architecture, programme management, governance, impact assessment
