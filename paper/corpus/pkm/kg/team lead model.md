# team lead model

Mental model for agentic software engineering: the engineer operates as team lead while agents perform execution. Agents handle the 80% (code generation, boilerplate, scoped subtasks); the engineer retains the 20% judgment work (architectural decisions, merge decisions, drift detection). Engineers who already worked as team leads or architects transition most naturally because they already practised context switching and reviewing output rather than writing it.

synonyms:: team-lead model, engineer as team lead, agentic team lead, delegation model, 80/20 agent delegation
