# ADM (Architecture Development Method)

The core of TOGAF -- a cyclic, iterative process for developing and managing enterprise architecture. The ADM provides a step-by-step approach through nine phases plus a central requirements management process.

Phases:
- Preliminary: establish architecture capability, principles, tools
- A (Architecture Vision): stakeholder analysis, scope, value chain
- B (Business Architecture): business strategy, organisation, processes
- C (Information Systems Architecture): data and application architecture
- D (Technology Architecture): technology standards, platforms
- E (Opportunities and Solutions): project identification, consolidation
- F (Migration Planning): implementation and migration plans
- G (Implementation Governance): architecture compliance during build
- H (Architecture Change Management): change management post-deployment
- Requirements Management: central process feeding all phases

Each phase produces specific catalogs, matrices, and diagrams as defined by the Content Metamodel.
