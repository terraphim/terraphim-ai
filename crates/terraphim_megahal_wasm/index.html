terraphim MegaHAL

A 1998 Loebner Prize chatterbot, ported to Rust and WebAssembly. Second-order Markov chains, deterministic seeded replies, zero LLM, zero network calls -- everything runs in your browser.

Original MegaHAL by Jason Hutchens (Unlicense). Rust port: terraphim_megahal.

Teach MegaHAL (one sentence per line)

MegaHAL is a 1998 Markov chatterbot, not an LLM: it recombines sentences from its training corpus, so it does not answer questions -- it improvises around your keywords. Use the Teach panel to train it, and switch personality above for themed brains (Star Trek knows Picard, Star Wars knows Luke).

Replies are generated by five second-order Markov models with seeded PCG32 randomisation: same seed plus same training always produces the same conversation. The brain is saved in the MHRS1W1 document format and never leaves this browser tab.
